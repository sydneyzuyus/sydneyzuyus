Needing outside talent isn’t a system glitch–it’s the norm. Today’s great agencies embrace and operationalize this reality. Since 2021, we’ve been empowering agencies to quickly and easily have access to the right freelance talent, stopping the hiring-firing cycle and burnout of existing staff that our industry has become famous for. Even with the move to project work, our scalable, seamless augmentation ensures that agencies can support their valuable employees while still improving profitability. We’re not just a stop-gap. We’re a best-in-class outside talent operating system that includes lightning-fast delivery, simple onboarding, agency culture briefings, and senior talent to oversee the work and relationship. It’s no wonder we’re loved by 50 agencies and counting.

TEAM MODEL
X
Everyone is collecting names, but no one is building teams. We see it every day. Agencies with ever-changing needs are relying on an internal freelance “roster” or “database” to find outside talent. And even if they do find the right resources, they are left with a lot of extra work: onboarding, aligning across workstreams, overseeing work product and fixing disconnects after the fact. Sounds more analog than modern to us! Thankfully, No Single Individual’s model disrupts this manual, cumbersome process. With our innovative team approach, every strategy and account ask is addressed by a curated team, including all the expertise, seniority levels and agency know-how needed to do the best work, quickly and profitably. And with the most-vetted talent and deepest freelance network around, we have the right people every time.
